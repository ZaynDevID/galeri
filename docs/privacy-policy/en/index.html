Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026 · Last updated 3 November 2026

In short: Galeri is a local, offline photo and video gallery. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, or internet permission. Your photos, videos, albums, favorites, PIN, and Trash remain on your device and are not sent anywhere.

1. Who operates this app

Galeri (package com.zayndev.galeri) is developed and published by ZaynDev. Privacy questions can be sent through the Developer email button on the app's Google Play listing.

2. Data the app accesses

  • Photos and videos on the device. With your permission, the app reads media from device storage to display, play, search, and manage it.
  • App settings. Theme, sorting, view mode, favorites, manual albums, and other preferences are stored locally.
  • PIN and album locks. The PIN is stored as a salted SHA-256 hash; the original PIN is not stored. Album lock state is also stored locally.
  • 30-day Trash. When you move media to Trash, the app copies the file into app-private storage before asking Android to delete the original MediaStore item. The copy can be restored or permanently deleted.
  • Crash logs. If the app crashes, technical details (including device model and Android version) may be written to the app's folder and/or Downloads/Galeri. Logs are not sent automatically and stay on your device.

The app does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, or browsing history.

3. Permissions used

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES and READ_MEDIA_VIDEO (Android 13+): read photos and videos.
  • READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED (Android 14+): allow access only to selected photos/videos when using limited access.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below): read media from storage.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 9 and below): restore media to Pictures/Galeri or Movies/Galeri.

Permissions are requested only when needed and can be revoked at any time in Android settings.

4. Collection and sharing

We do not collect, sell, rent, or share personal data. The app uses no advertising SDK, analytics, tracking, or third-party service that sends data. No data is sent to our servers because the app has no internet connection.

The Share feature only passes the files you select to another Android app through an intent after you initiate it. You choose the destination, and that app has its own privacy policy.

5. Storage and deletion

Settings, favorites, albums, PIN, and Trash metadata are stored on the device until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Original media is not modified or deleted unless you choose a delete action and approve Android's confirmation. Trash items are kept for at most 30 days and are then automatically cleaned when the app runs. You can restore or permanently delete them sooner from Trash.

Clearing app data deletes Trash and local settings. Crash logs in Downloads/Galeri can be deleted with a file manager.

6. Security

Because data is not sent off the device, risk mainly depends on your device security. The app PIN and album locks are local UI protections, not encryption of media files. Keep your screen lock enabled and back up important files separately.

7. Children

This app is not specifically directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal data. It only displays media already on the device or selected by the user.

8. External links

If you open a link or another app, that party's privacy policy applies. The app does not send data to a browser or website.

9. Policy changes

If data practices change, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised. The latest version is always available at this address.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, corrections, or requests, use the Developer email button on the Galeri Google Play listing. We will respond through the channel available there.
